### CLIM-000002: Historical-to-future pattern-effect correction
- **Subfield:** Radiative forcing, clouds, and climate sensitivity
- **Open:** Predict how geographical patterns of ocean warming change global radiative feedback. Infer long-term sensitivity from historical observations without prescribing the uncertain future sea-surface-temperature pattern.
- **Status:** Intensive work since the 2010s demonstrates substantial historical effective-sensitivity biases in model experiments; the real-world correction remains uncertain.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Connects observed warming rates to future warming.
- **Key works:** Gregory et al. (2020; online 2019), [“How accurately can the climate sensitivity to CO₂ be estimated from historical climate change?”](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00382-019-04991-y).
- **Related:** System identification with time-varying parameters; causal transportability.
- **Aliases:** SST pattern effect.
